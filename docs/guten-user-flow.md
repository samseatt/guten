## **🔹 User Flow: Publishing on Guten.ink** 🚀  

Below is the structured **user journey from onboarding to AI-powered publishing** on Guten.ink.  

---

### **📌 User Flow Breakdown**  

#### **🛠️ Step 1: Onboarding & Account Creation**  
- **User visits Guten.ink**  
- **Options to sign up** as:  
  ✔ **Individual contributor (Pneuma, researcher, writer, journalist, educator, SME)**  
  ✔ **Organization (Adaptive enterprise, university, research group, institution, business, Mesh entity)**  
  ✔ **Publisher (Media house, digital publication, decentralized knowledge community, etc.)**  
- **User selects publishing preferences:**  
  ✔ Open Knowledge (Creative Commons / Public Contribution)  
  ✔ Private (Internal Organization Publishing)  
  ✔ Peer-Reviewed & Governed (Decentralized Governance Model)  
- **AI-assisted profile creation** (tailored user experience based on expertise, interests, and goals).  

---

#### **🛠️ Step 2: Creating a New Publishing Space (Optional for Organizations/Communities)**  
- **Users can create their own sub-domain or namespace on Guten.ink**  
  ✔ Example: **org.guten.ink** or **journal.guten.ink**  
  ✔ Configure governance settings (who can submit, who can review, etc.)  
  ✔ Set visibility (public, restricted, gated)  
  ✔ Connect to **Neubank for rewards & recognition tracking (Neummus incentives)**  

---

#### **🛠️ Step 3: Creating a New Page or Publication**  
- **User selects publishing type:**  
  ✔ **Article / Research Paper / Journal Entry**  
  ✔ **Knowledge Contribution (Semantic Insight, FAQs, Public Policy Paper, Analysis)**  
  ✔ **Educational Content (Course Module, Open Learning, Lesson Plan)**  
  ✔ **Live Knowledge Threads (Collaborative publishing in a research space)**  
- **User is prompted with an AI-assisted form to describe the submission.**  
  ✔ Title, Description, Tags  
  ✔ Context & References  
  ✔ Desired **AI Structuring & Semantic Interlinking Preferences**  

---

#### **🛠️ Step 4: AI-Powered Content Curation & Structuring**  
- **AI generates an initial structured draft**  
  ✔ Suggests **section breakdowns, content flow, and references**  
  ✔ **Automatically interlinks knowledge** to other related Guten.ink publications  
  ✔ Creates **semantic tags & categories**  
- **User can:**  
  ✔ Accept AI structuring  
  ✔ Modify & edit sections  
  ✔ Request further refinements  
  ✔ Choose governance flow (peer review, automatic AI validation, open publishing)  

---

#### **🛠️ Step 5: Peer Review & Governance (Optional)**  
- **If user opts for review:**  
  ✔ AI assigns the submission to relevant governance Meshes & SMEs  
  ✔ Reviewers leave structured feedback  
  ✔ AI adapts and refines based on corrections & suggestions  

---

#### **🛠️ Step 6: Publishing & Recognition**  
- **Final approval step:**  
  ✔ User confirms final version  
  ✔ AI generates **summary, key insights, and structured metadata**  
  ✔ Content is published & indexed into **the Guten.ink Knowledge Graph**  
  ✔ Users receive **Neummus-based recognition & reputation tracking**  
- **Published work becomes part of the AI-powered Guten.ink Knowledge Network.**  

---
